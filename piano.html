Piano — Support

For iPhone and iPad

Piano is a grand piano for touch screens: all 88 keys, scales and modes that switch off the notes you don't need, a sustain pedal, multitouch chords and glissando.

Questions, bug reports or ideas — email i@jenyadoesapps.com. Please mention your device and iOS version if something isn't working.

Tips

  • Louder or softer: touch near the front edge of a key for a full note, near the back for a softer one.
  • Scales: tap KEY and SCALE to lock the keyboard to a scale; keys outside it are dimmed and silent. Choose "All" to play every key again.
  • Sustain: tap SUSTAIN to hold notes after you lift your fingers; tap it again to release them.
  • More or fewer keys: use the zoom buttons; the arrows move by one key or by an octave.
  • iPad: the keyboard always runs along the long side of the screen — turn the iPad sideways for the most keys.
  • No sound? Turn up the volume. Piano plays even with the silent switch on, so if it's still quiet, check that audio isn't going to a connected Bluetooth device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers the Piano app for iPhone and iPad by jenyadoesapps ("we", "us"). In short: Piano does not collect any data about you.

1. Data we collect

None. Piano has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs. The app works entirely offline and does not send anything over the internet.

2. Data stored on your device

Piano remembers your preferences — the selected key and scale, zoom level, keyboard position and whether sustain is on — so they are the same next time you open it. These settings stay on your device, are never sent to us, and are removed when you delete the app.

3. Permissions

Piano only plays sound. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

4. Apple

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports. These do not identify you. Apple's handling of data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

Piano is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: i@jenyadoesapps.com.
